## Program Overview
- **Goal:** Improve delivery of cost-effective, coordinated, high-quality healthcare in **rural primary care settings**
- **Funding:** Up to **$228,000/year**, 4-year performance period
- **Administered by:** Federal Office of Rural Health Policy (within HRSA)
- **Aligned with:** HHS priority "Making America Healthy Again"
- **Volume:** 240 applications received (120 expected). Summary statements required.
- **Assignments:** ~5 applications max per reviewer

## Three Program Goals
1. Prepare rural healthcare providers to develop **sustained quality improvement strategies**
2. Strengthen organizational capacity for **collecting/analyzing data** and using technology tools
3. Build **financial sustainability** through workforce training, billing, coding, and reimbursement strategies

## Clinical Health Service Focus Areas (applicant picks one)
- Care coordination
- Practice management
- Integrating behavioral health into primary care
- Pulmonary or cardiac rehab
- Other (must align with improving health outcomes with potential for sustained services post-funding)

## Required Implementation Areas (all applicants must address all 3)
1. Implement quality improvement strategies and train staff for sustained QI culture
2. Implement value-based care strategies
3. Implement billing/coding strategies to drive increased revenue from new/expanded services

## Eligibility (already verified by program)
- Domestic public or nonprofit entity in a **rural area**
- Must serve population within **HRSA-designated rural area**
- Must be a **provider of healthcare services**

## Scoring Rules
- **Whole numbers only** — no fractions
- Use the scoring rubric as guideline
- Scores must reflect S/M/W comments
- **Do not give zeros** — minimum score if criteria is addressed at all
- Comments must reference **application page numbers**
- Refer to "**the application**" not "the applicant"
- **Do not compare applications** (especially same-state apps)
- **No outside information** — score on application merit only

## Comment Types

| Type | Definition |
|------|-----------|
| **Strength** | Response clearly meets or substantially exceeds NOFO requirements |
| **Met** | Addresses basic/minimal requirements of the NOFO. Don't just say "met" — describe **which bullets/criterion** were met |
| **Weakness** | Avoid prescriptive language ("should have", "could have been strengthened by"). Describe what's missing/insufficient |

## Writing Guidance
- Write **complete, comprehensive, continuous** statements
- Spell out acronyms on first use
- Use data/documentation from the application
- Don't retype the applicant's introduction
- Separate comments by criteria — **don't bunch everything together**
- Read the **reviewer tip sheet** before starting

## Budget Review (Criterion 6) — Eric Brown, GMS

### Assessment Criteria
- Costs are **reasonable and aligned** with proposed goals
- Budget clearly supports program objectives
- Budget is reasonable **for each year** of performance period
- **Cost sharing should NOT be considered** (not a cost-sharing program)
- **PD salary cap:** $228,000

### Common Budget Discrepancy
- SF-424 Section 18, SF-424A budget form, and budget justification may not match
- **Use the total closest to the funding ceiling** when discrepancies exist
- **Document all discrepancies** in critique and consult GMS

### Budget Reductions
- Provide **specific dollar amounts** with associated line items
- Provide **clear rationale** for each reduction
- Reductions must be **consistent across all budget years**
- Consider how reductions impact applicant's ability to achieve goals
- Final budget determinations made by GMS, but they review panel consensus

### Note-to-HER Items (flag but don't score)
- Calculation discrepancies
- Missing indirect cost rate agreements
- Salary above $228,000 cap
- Questionable allowability of costs

## ORC Process
- Chair starts with **weaknesses first** for each criterion
- All panel members score **all applications** on their panel (not just assigned ones)
- Listen carefully during other reviewers' discussions — you score based on discussion
- Chair does **not submit evaluations or scores** but reads all applications
- Summary statement operators capture discussion in real-time
- Once submitted to chair, **cannot edit** — must call to have it reopened
